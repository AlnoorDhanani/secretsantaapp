# Privacy Policy

**Secret Santa App**
*Last updated: February 2026*

## What We Collect

Secret Santa App collects the following information when provided by the event organizer:

- **Participant names** — used to create gift exchange assignments
- **Phone numbers (optional)** — used solely to deliver Secret Santa assignment notifications via SMS

## How We Use Your Information

- **Names** are used to generate and display Secret Santa assignments within the app.
- **Phone numbers**, when provided, are used only to send a one-time SMS message notifying each participant of their Secret Santa assignment.

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to the Secret Santa gift exchange.

## SMS Messaging

- **Message content**: A single notification containing your Secret Santa assignment.
- **Message frequency**: One (1) message per gift exchange event. No recurring messages.
- **Opt-out**: Reply **STOP** to any message to opt out of future messages.
- **Help**: Reply **HELP** for assistance.
- **Message and data rates may apply** depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

## Data Storage

- All data is stored locally on the organizer's device.
- No data is transmitted to external servers except for the SMS delivery service (Twilio) when the organizer chooses to send SMS notifications.
- Phone numbers sent to Twilio are used solely for message delivery and are subject to [Twilio's Privacy Policy](https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy).

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Phone numbers are shared with Twilio only for the purpose of delivering SMS notifications.

## Data Retention

- Draw data (names, assignments) is stored locally and can be deleted by the organizer at any time.
- We do not retain any data on remote servers.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the [project repository](https://github.com/AlnoorDhanani/secretsantaapp).
